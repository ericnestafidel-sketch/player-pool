# Coach Code of Conduct

**Last updated November 2026**

**Effective November 9, 2026**

Player Pool exists to help families find the right travel team, and to keep their kids safe while they do. Every Coach must agree to this Code before we approve access, and must follow it the whole time they use Player Pool. It is part of our [Terms of Use](/terms).

**Breaking this Code can mean immediate suspension or permanent removal.** Where a child's safety may be at risk, it can also mean a report to the authorities. A report from a Parent suspends your access automatically while we review it.

This Code follows the same principles as the U.S. Center for SafeSport's Minor Athlete Abuse Prevention Policies (MAAPP). If your organization, league, or governing body has stricter rules, follow those too.

---

## By using Player Pool, I agree that:

### Who I am
1. **I am who I say I am.** My name, team, organization, and references are true, and I'll keep them current. I won't let anyone else use my account.
2. **I'm eligible to work with youth athletes.** I have not been convicted of, or charged and awaiting trial for, any crime involving a child, sexual misconduct, or violence. I am not on any sex offender registry. I am not suspended, banned, or under sanction by the U.S. Center for SafeSport or any sports organization. If any of that changes, I'll stop using Player Pool and tell Player Pool right away.
3. **I'll meet my organization's safety requirements**, including any background check and SafeSport (or similar) abuse-prevention training it requires, and I'll keep them current.

### How I contact families
4. **I contact Parents only, never the player.** All first contact goes through Player Pool's in-app messages to the Parent. I will never try to contact a player directly: no texts, calls, emails, social media follows or messages, gaming or app chats, or approaching them in person.
5. **No one-on-one contact with a minor.** Any later communication with a player (for example after they join my team) will include the parent or another adult, following my organization's rules. I won't have private, one-on-one in-person, phone, or online contact with a minor I found through Player Pool.
6. **In-person meetings happen in the open.** Tryouts, workouts, and meetings happen at open team activities or public places, with the Parent present or nearby and informed. I will never transport a player, or meet a player privately, alone, or at a home.
7. **I respect "no."** If a Parent declines, blocks me, or doesn't reply, I won't contact them again about that player, on or off Player Pool.
8. **I'm honest in my outreach.** I'll be clear about my team, level, fees, travel commitment, schedule, and tryout process. I won't make promises I can't keep, such as guaranteed playing time, positions, or exposure.

### How I treat player information
9. **I won't try to identify or locate a player** beyond what the Parent chooses to share. I won't search for the player's name, school, home, or social media.
10. **I keep profiles private.** I won't screenshot, copy, download, post, or share player information with anyone except my own team's coaching staff, for that team's roster decisions, and never publicly or for any other purpose.
11. **I use Player Pool only for youth baseball team placement.** No selling, marketing lessons or camps, recruiting for other businesses, or surveys.

### What I say and do
12. **My messages stay professional and about baseball.** No sexual, romantic, or personal comments about any child. No pressure tactics, threats, insults, or harassment.
13. **No gifts, money, or special favors** to a player or family to gain an advantage, and **no requests for photos or videos** beyond the highlight links the Parent chose to share.
14. **No alcohol, drugs, or tobacco** around players in anything connected to Player Pool, and no inappropriate conduct of any kind toward a minor.

### Reporting
15. **I'll report concerns right away.** If I see or suspect anything unsafe for a child, I'll report it to Player Pool. **If I have reason to believe a child is being abused or neglected, I'll report it to the authorities.** Call your state's child abuse hotline or 1-800-422-4453 Childhelp; in Indiana, DCS 1-800-800-5556. Call **911** in an emergency. I'll also report sexual misconduct to the U.S. Center for SafeSport where it applies (uscenterforsafesport.org).
16. **I'll cooperate** with any Player Pool review and with law enforcement or child-protection investigations.

### Enforcement
17. **I understand that Player Pool may:**
    - suspend me automatically when a Parent reports me, and keep me suspended while it reviews;
    - remove me at any time;
    - keep records of reports and removals to keep removed Coaches off the site;
    - share information with law enforcement, child-protection agencies, the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children, the U.S. Center for SafeSport, or my organization when a child's safety may be at risk.

---

**Agreement.** By checking the box on the Coach sign-up form, I confirm that I've read this Code, that my answers are true, and that I agree to follow it. My agreement is recorded with the date and the version of this Code.
